Kenya Cedant Scorecard

Every general insurer in Kenya graded each year the way a reinsurance underwriter screens a ceding company: balance sheet, underwriting, the shape of the book, and whether the numbers can be trusted. Built only from the Insurance Regulatory Authority's published returns.

Left out of

Micro insurers are not scored. Neither is any insurer below KSh 500m of written premium, or without four quarterly returns for the year.

Method

How a grade is built

The structure follows the building blocks of Best's Credit Rating Methodology, cut down to what IRA publishes. Each metric is ranked against that year's peers on a 0 to 100 percentile, averaged within its pillar and weighted. Return quality is an absolute score: 100, less 30 for each return that fails a consistency check, 10 for each warning and 10 for each missed filing over three years.

Grades: A 70 and above, B 55 to 70, C 40 to 55, D 25 to 40, E below 25. Negative equity, or equity under IRA's minimum capital, caps the grade at D whatever the score.

  • These are relative screening grades, not credit ratings. They say where an insurer stood among its Kenyan peers that year.
  • IRA's quarterly returns are unaudited. The balance sheet is the general business return, so composites show their general fund only.
  • Equity stands in for IRA's risk-based capital test, which needs data IRA does not publish. It is the lenient side of that test: IRA counted 15 of 35 general insurers short of capital at December 2023; the floor here flags 9 of 34.
  • The floor applies today's rule to every year. For 2016 to 2019 that is stricter than the rule then in force and caps six grades, none of them in the back-test.
  • IRA moved the statements to IFRS 17 from Q1 2024. Ranking within each year keeps comparisons like for like.
  • IRA has no year-end 2017 workbook online, so 2017 is blank.

Source: Insurance Regulatory Authority, Kenya, quarterly industry statistics, Q4 2016 to Q1 2026, as extracted and validated in ira-kenya-data. Regulator actions: Capital FM (Resolution, April 2022; Xplico and Invesco, 8 December 2023; Invesco again, August 2024), IRA on X (Trident and Corporate, March 2026), Business Daily (capital shortfalls, 8 December 2024; Kuscco Mutual, April 2026). Everything here is public data. Nothing comes from inside any insurer.
